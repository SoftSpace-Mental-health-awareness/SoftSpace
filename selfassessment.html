🌿 Emotional Well-Being Self-Assessment

Answer simple questions to reflect on your emotional well-being.
There are no right or wrong answers — just be honest with yourself 💙

1. How often do you feel calm and relaxed during the day?
2. How often do you experience feelings of anxiety or worry?
3. How well do you manage negative emotions (anger, sadness, frustration)?
4. How often do you take time for self-care or relaxation?
5. How connected do you feel with your friends or family?
6. How often do you feel proud or content with yourself?
7. How often do you feel emotionally exhausted?
8. How often do you engage in activities that make you happy?
9. Do you feel hopeful about the future?
10. How often do you practice mindfulness or deep breathing?
